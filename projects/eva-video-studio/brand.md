_Status: **draft, pending onboarding and owner review.** Only facts seen on 2026-11-27 are listed._

## What it is
An AI video style template service. Visitors browse EVA video style templates, download free open-source styles,
unlock Premium styles with a Membership, or edit videos online with AI in the Studio.
_Source: evavideostudio.com home page title "EVA Video Studio — AI video style templates" and its meta description._

The open-source engine ("EVA Core") is published from the public repo `RoverEva/eva-video-style`. The hosted Studio
(this project) is a separate private SaaS. _Source: `RoverEva/Eva-Video-Studio/README.md`._
