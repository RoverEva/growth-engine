## Plans (per student)
| Plan | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Set 1 of every program, with a parent account |
| All-Access Monthly | $19.99/month | 7-day free trial ($0 today), cancel anytime |
| All-Access Yearly | $129.99/year (≈ $10.83/month, about 46% less than monthly) | 7-day free trial |

All-Access includes every program and level. Choosing a test only personalizes the content. Access is per student.

## Features
Flashcards, synonyms, sentence completion, analogies, smart review, six learning games (Stoke the Fire, Dragon
Flight Path, Forge the Dragon Scale, Term & Definition Match, Hatchery Match-Up, Dragon's Whisper), Dragon Studio,
coins and dragon rewards, parent learning-report emails, Refer & Earn credits, Google sign-in (added 2026-11-27).
