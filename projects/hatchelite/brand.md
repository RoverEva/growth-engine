# HatchElite: brand

_Status: reviewed by the owner in chat 2026-11-26/27; facts below cite their source. Last updated 2026-11-27._

## What it is
A vocabulary and test-prep web app for students in Grades 2–12, preparing for the ISEE, SSAT and Digital SAT.
Students study word sets (flashcards, synonyms, sentence completion, analogies) and play learning games, and they earn
coins to hatch and raise dragons. One parent account manages billing and several student profiles.
_Source: hatchelite.com home page and `hatchelite-v2-clean/src/routes/HomePage.jsx`, `AuthPage.jsx`._

## Positioning
- Tagline on the home page: **"Learn. Hatch. Grow."**
- Promise: "Master vocabulary, reading, and test prep through fun study paths, games, and dragon rewards."
- Differentiators: gamified practice (Game Zone, Dragon Studio, coins and eggs), levels matched to each test, smart
  review of missed words, per-student progress in one parent account.

## Audience
- **Primary buyer:** parents of students applying to independent/private schools (ISEE, SSAT) or preparing for the
  Digital SAT, plus parents who want grade-level vocabulary practice.
- **User:** the student (Grades 2–12).
- **Segment to test (hypothesis, unverified):** Chinese-American families preparing for ISEE/SSAT; reach via
  小红书 / WeChat in Simplified Chinese.

## Voice
Warm, practical, specific. Plain English. No hype. Written for busy parents. Facts about tests come from official
sources (admission.org / E3n, erblearn.org, ssat.org, College Board).
